Why these exist

Everything else here is analysis: a question asked and answered. These two are reference material, written to be useful to someone who was not here. The test is whether a new product manager, in their first week, can get a real answer out of one without asking anybody and without being confidently misled.

Three things a handover guide has to do

State your confidence on every claim

In the repo guide, every owner is labeled documented, guessed or disputed, and the 271 repositories it could not sort are shown as unsorted rather than forced into a group. A guide that hides what it does not know gets trusted during an outage and sends someone to the wrong place.

Teach the question, not just the answer

Every entry in the non-functional guide comes with the questions to ask engineering and the wording to write down. Neither guide tries to make you an expert. They try to make your next conversation a competent one.

Hosting is most of the value

Both are single files, which is why they work offline and why they can vanish at handover. Both now run inside their own page here. What is still missing is an internal link someone can open in the middle of an outage.
