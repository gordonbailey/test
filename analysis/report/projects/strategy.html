Two gaps, one through-line

The growth argument underneath the project work. Both gaps are versions of the same problem: the platform monetises moments of generosity, but does not build generous people. Donation sizes are up while donor counts are flat to down, which is growth getting narrower rather than growth.

Gap 01

We haven't made giving cool

Giving here is episodic — donors show up for a crisis and disappear. There is no identity, status or habit attached to being generous. Marketplace is a first attempt, but "does this make giving cool?" should be a lens on every product decision rather than one product's job.

Gap 02

We under-invest in super donors

Donors giving 2+ times a year produce 39% of GDV from 20% of the donor base — and get the same experience as a first-time donor. No owner, no lifecycle engagement, no recognition.

What the data confirmed, and what it redirected

The argument was taken to Amplitude rather than accepted or rejected on its face. Three parts held, two changed.

Read the limits before quoting any of this

Nothing in this branch is causal

Every figure describes what different donor populations already do. None of it shows that an intervention moves a donor between bands — the bands may simply contain different kinds of people. The dollar figures are the size of an observed gap, not a measured treatment effect. Each page's "pick this up next" leads with the experiment that would fix this, because it is the same gap everywhere.

The strongest cells are the smallest

The 28.3% chat referral rate is n=699 against a 20.2M baseline. Directional colour, not a forecastable rate. Only a handful of channels carry planning-grade volume.

41% of donors cannot be attributed at all

8.3M donors carry no referral medium, and the field is polluted by Meta ad-set IDs and case duplicates. Every channel comparison here describes instrumented traffic rather than all traffic — the consumer-side face of exactly what the UTM project exists to fix.

Three GDV totals are in circulation

$1.55B in the working spreadsheet, $1.74B in the deck, $1.775B from Amplitude. The shares are stable across all three so the 20/39 argument is safe, but the absolute dollars need reconciling to one source. Detailed on the GDV page.
