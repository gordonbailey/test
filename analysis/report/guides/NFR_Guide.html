Field Guide · Non-Functional Requirements

The qualities that decide whether a release survives production.

A working feature answers "what does it do?" The non-functional requirements decide whether it holds up under real conditions — speed, reliability, safety, and the cost of changing it later. This guide turns each one into a plain definition, the questions to ask engineering, the spec language, and the tradeoffs a PM needs to reason about them.
